# How Edits Stay Attached to Content

> This document explains the IncuBrix timeline model.
> It will be expanded as the engine is implemented.

## Key Principle

Edits belong to **content**, not to a clock position.

A caption added on the words "welcome to the channel" stays on those exact
words after anything before it is cut, sped up, reordered, or restored.
Overlays and effects store **word IDs**. Output times are *computed* from
the current timeline every time.
